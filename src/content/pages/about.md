I am a third-year law student at Fordham Law. I focus on digital assets, DeFi, and AI. My coursework covers blockchain, smart contracts, and DeFi alongside securities regulation, corporate finance, and securitization.

My note, *Registered Agents of the DAO*, starts from the gap between liability and enforcement. Courts treat DAOs as general partnerships, but the doctrine reaches only defendants visible enough to find, so *Samuels v. Lido DAO* caught prominent VCs while the protocol's architects went unreachable. The note proposes a federal framework: DAOs with a U.S. nexus designate a registered agent and maintain a liquid reserve fund, and exchanges that list noncompliant governance tokens face independent liability. It is forthcoming in the Fordham Journal of Corporate and Financial Law in 2027. I am also Senior Notes Editor of the Journal.

This semester I am a law clerk at the Wyoming Stable Token Commission, supporting the legal and compliance frameworks for the Frontier (FRNT) Token, the first fiat-backed, fully reserved stable token issued by a U.S. public entity. In the spring I will join the DeFi Education Fund as a legal extern, supporting its DeFi policy research and regulatory advocacy before Congress and federal agencies.

I also work on legal AI. I founded Legal IQ and prototyped an AI-powered legal compliance platform for early-stage startups. This past summer, as a law clerk at Fidelity National Financial, I developed AI-assisted research and document analysis workflows. I am now Fordham's student ambassador for Harvey, the legal AI platform.
